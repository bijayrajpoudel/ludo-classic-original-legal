# Privacy Policy — Ludo Original Classic

_Effective date: 19 October 2026 · Applies to app version 1.2.3 and later_

**In short:** Ludo Original Classic is an offline board game. It has no accounts, no ads, no in-app purchases, no
analytics and no tracking. The app does not collect, send, sell or share any personal information. Your settings and
your saved game stay on your own device.

## 1. Who we are

The app is made and published by **Bijay Raj Poudel**, an individual developer in Nepal ("we", "us"). On Google Play
the developer name is **Roulette Wheel**. Contact: **poudelbijayraj500@gmail.com**.

For the purposes of data protection laws (such as the EU and UK GDPR), we would be the "controller" of any personal
data we receive. In practice, the only personal data we can ever receive is what you choose to send us by email
(section 7).

## 2. What this policy covers

This policy covers the Ludo Original Classic app on Android (package `com.bijayraj.ludoimperia`) and, if released,
on iOS. It does not cover app stores, your device's operating system, or websites the app opens (section 6), which
have their own privacy policies.

## 3. Information the app does NOT collect

The app:
- has no accounts, sign-in or sign-up;
- does not ask for or read your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, photos, files, camera or
  microphone;
- contains no advertising, analytics, tracking, attribution, social or crash-reporting SDKs;
- does not use the advertising ID or any other device identifier;
- has no online play, chat or leaderboards: every game is played on your device (against the computer or with
  people passing the same phone);
- does not send any information about you or your games to us or to anyone else.

We have switched off the game engine's (Unity's) optional usage statistics, analytics and crash-report features for
this app.

## 4. Information saved only on your device

So that you can continue where you left off, the app saves the following **only on your device**:
- your settings: house rules, sound effects, vibration, game speed, auto-move options, dice mode, computer level, and
  your chosen board, dice and coin style;
- the player set-up, including any **player names** you choose to type (optional; names are never required);
- the game in progress, so you can CONTINUE it later;
- small counters the game uses for itself, such as how many games have been finished and which first-game tips you
  have already seen.

This data is not sent to us or to anyone else. We cannot see it.

**How long it is kept:** until you change it, use RESET TO DEFAULTS in Settings, clear the app's storage in your
phone's settings, or uninstall the app. Uninstalling deletes everything the app saved.

**Device backups:** if you have turned on your phone's own backup service (for example Google's Android backup), your
phone may include the app's saved settings and game in your personal backup. That backup is handled by your device
and its provider under your account and settings; we never receive it.

## 5. Permissions

- **Vibration** — a short buzz on a six, a cut or a coin reaching home. You can switch it off in Settings
  (Vibration). It gives the app no access to any information.
- **Network access (Android "INTERNET" permission)** — the Unity game engine adds this standard permission to the
  app package automatically. It is a "normal" permission that Android grants without asking. The game does not use it
  to send any personal information, and it has no online features, ads or analytics.

The app asks for no other permissions. It does not request location, contacts, storage, camera or microphone access.

## 6. Buttons that leave the app

Some buttons hand you over to other apps or services, and only when you tap them:
- **RATE THIS APP** opens the app's page in your app store;
- **SHARE** opens your phone's share sheet with a short invitation text and a store link; you choose whether and
  with whom to share it;
- **PRIVACY POLICY** (OPEN FULL POLICY) opens this policy in your browser;
- an email you send to us is sent by your own email app.

What happens there is covered by those services' own privacy policies (for example Google's or Apple's), not by this
one. The app does not learn what you do there.

## 7. If you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address, your name if your email shows it, and whatever you write. We use it
only to answer you and to fix problems you report. We do not add you to a mailing list, sell it or share it. We keep
the conversation only as long as needed to help you, and in any case delete it within 24 months unless the law
requires otherwise; you can ask us to delete it sooner. Our email is hosted by Google (Gmail), so the message is
stored on Google's servers, which may be outside your country.

Legal basis (GDPR / UK GDPR): our legitimate interest in answering your message (Article 6(1)(f)), or taking steps you
asked for (Article 6(1)(b)).

## 8. Children

The game contains no personal data collection, no chat, no online play, no ads and no purchases, so it is suitable
for players of all ages. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children
under 13 (US COPPA), under 16 (EU GDPR, depending on the country) or of any other age. Player names typed in the game
stay on the device.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has sent us personal information by email, contact us and we
will delete it.

## 9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as the **EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)**, the **UK GDPR and
Data Protection Act 2018**, the **California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act
(CCPA/CPRA)** and **Nepal's Individual Privacy Act, 2075 (2018)** give you rights over your personal data, such as
the right to know what is held, to access, correct or delete it, to restrict or object to its use, to data
portability, and not to be discriminated against for using these rights.

- **The app itself:** we hold no personal data from the app, so there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or
  delete. All game data is under your own control on your device (section 4).
- **Emails you sent us:** you may ask for a copy, a correction or deletion at any time by writing to
  poudelbijayraj500@gmail.com. We will reply within one month (GDPR / UK GDPR) or 45 days (CCPA/CPRA).
- **California (CCPA/CPRA):** in the last 12 months we have not sold or "shared" (for cross-context behavioural
  advertising) any personal information, and we do not use sensitive personal information. We collect no categories of
  personal information through the app; the only category we may receive is identifiers and message content in emails
  you choose to send.
- **Nepal (Individual Privacy Act, 2075):** we do not collect, store, process or disclose personal information through
  the app. Information you send us by email is used only for the purpose you sent it for (answering you), is not
  disclosed to anyone else without your consent except as required by law, and is deleted as described in section 7.
- **Complaints:** you may also complain to your local data protection authority (for example your EU supervisory
  authority or the UK Information Commissioner's Office). We would appreciate the chance to answer your concern first.

## 10. Security

No personal data leaves your device through the app, so it cannot be exposed by a breach of our systems. The settings
and saved game are kept in the app's private storage, protected by your device's own security. Keep your device
updated and locked.

## 11. Advertising and purchases

The app has no advertising and no in-app purchases. If we ever add ads, purchases or any feature that collects data,
we will update this policy, the store's data safety information and, where required, ask for your consent **before**
that version is released.

## 12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The new version will be published at the same address with a new
effective date. Important changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.
