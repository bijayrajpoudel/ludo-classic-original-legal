# Privacy Policy — Ludo Classic Original

_Effective date: 26 November 2026 · Applies to app version 1.2.20 and later_

**In short:** Ludo Classic Original is a board game you play on your device. It has no accounts, no ads and no in-app
purchases. From version 1.2.20 the Android app uses **Google Firebase Analytics** to count how many people play and in
which countries (section 3). It does not use the advertising ID, and we never sell your data. Your settings and your
saved game stay on your own device.

## 1. Who we are

The app is made and published by **Bijay Raj Poudel**, an individual developer in Nepal ("we", "us"). On Google Play
the developer name is **Roulette Wheel**. Contact: **poudelbijayraj500@gmail.com**.

For the purposes of data protection laws (such as the EU and UK GDPR), we would be the "controller" of any personal
data we receive. In practice, the only personal data we can ever receive is what you choose to send us by email
(section 8).

## 2. What this policy covers

This policy covers the Ludo Classic Original app on Android (package `com.bijayraj.ludoimperia`) and, if released,
on iOS. It does not cover app stores, your device's operating system, or websites the app opens (section 7), which
have their own privacy policies.

## 3. Usage statistics (Google Firebase Analytics)

From version 1.2.20 the Android app uses Google Firebase Analytics, a service of Google LLC, so that we can see how
many people play and in which countries. Firebase collects automatically:
- an app-instance ID (a random number Firebase creates for this install; it is not your name, phone number or
  advertising ID, and it is reset if you uninstall the app or clear its data);
- when the app is first opened, how often and how long it is used, and the app version;
- device model, Android version, language and screen size;
- your approximate country and region, which Google works out from your network connection (no GPS or precise
  location).

The game also tells Firebase when a game starts, ends or is left early, with the board, die and coin style used,
how many people and computers play, whether a person or a computer won, and how many minutes the game took. The game
does not send your player names, the moves of your games or anything you type to Firebase. We use this information only
in totals (for example "120 players in Nepal this week") to understand how the game is used and to improve it. We do
not sell it or use it for advertising. Google processes it under the
[Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and the
[Firebase data processing terms](https://firebase.google.com/terms/data-processing-terms); it may be processed on
Google's servers outside your country. Firebase keeps this data for 2 months, after which it is deleted.

Legal basis (GDPR / UK GDPR): our legitimate interest in understanding how many people use the game and where, so we
can improve it (Article 6(1)(f)). You can object at any time by writing to us (section 14).

## 4. Information the app does NOT collect

The app:
- has no accounts, sign-in or sign-up;
- does not ask for or read your name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise location, photos, files, camera
  or microphone;
- contains no advertising, attribution, social or crash-reporting SDKs;
- does not use the advertising ID (advertising-ID collection is switched off and its permission is removed);
- has no online play, chat or leaderboards: every game is played on your device (against the computer or with
  people passing the same phone).

We have switched off the game engine's (Unity's) optional usage statistics, analytics and crash-report features for
this app.

## 5. Information saved only on your device

So that you can continue where you left off, the app saves the following **only on your device**:
- your settings: house rules, sound effects, vibration, game speed, auto-move options, dice mode, computer level, and
  your chosen board, dice and coin style;
- the player set-up, including any **player names** you choose to type (optional; names are never required);
- the game in progress, so you can CONTINUE it later;
- small counters the game uses for itself, such as how many games have been finished and which first-game tips you
  have already seen.

This data is not sent to us or to anyone else. We cannot see it.

**How long it is kept:** until you change it, use RESET TO DEFAULTS in Settings, clear the app's storage in your
phone's settings, or uninstall the app. Uninstalling deletes everything the app saved.

**Device backups:** if you have turned on your phone's own backup service (for example Google's Android backup), your
phone may include the app's saved settings and game in your personal backup. That backup is handled by your device
and its provider under your account and settings; we never receive it.

## 6. Permissions

- **Vibration** — a short buzz on a six, a cut or a coin reaching home. You can switch it off in Settings
  (Vibration). It gives the app no access to any information.

- **Internet and network state** (Android, from version 1.2.20) — used only by Firebase Analytics to send the usage
  statistics in section 3. The game itself works offline.

The app asks for no other permissions. It does not request location, contacts, storage, camera or microphone access.

## 7. Buttons that leave the app

Some buttons hand you over to other apps or services, and only when you tap them:
- **RATE THIS APP** opens the app's page in your app store;
- **SHARE** opens your phone's share sheet with a short invitation text and a store link; you choose whether and
  with whom to share it;
- **PRIVACY POLICY** (OPEN FULL POLICY) opens this policy in your browser;
- an email you send to us is sent by your own email app.

**Rating card.** After you win a game, the app may ask Google Play to show its own rating card (at most once every 60
days). The card is shown and handled entirely by Google Play: if you choose to leave a star rating or a review, you
send it to Google Play under Google's terms and privacy policy, it is not sent to us through the app, and the app is
not told whether or how you rated. You can close the card without rating.

What happens there is covered by those services' own privacy policies (for example Google's or Apple's), not by this
one. The app does not learn what you do there.

## 8. If you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address, your name if your email shows it, and whatever you write. We use it
only to answer you and to fix problems you report. We do not add you to a mailing list, sell it or share it. We keep
the conversation only as long as needed to help you, and in any case delete it within 24 months unless the law
requires otherwise; you can ask us to delete it sooner. Our email is hosted by Google (Gmail), so the message is
stored on Google's servers, which may be outside your country.

Legal basis (GDPR / UK GDPR): our legitimate interest in answering your message (Article 6(1)(f)), or taking steps you
asked for (Article 6(1)(b)).

## 9. Children

The game has no chat, no online play, no ads and no purchases. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not
knowingly collect personal information from children. The only data that leaves the device is the usage statistics
in section 3, which contain no names or contact details. Player names typed in the game stay on the device.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has sent us personal information by email, contact us and we
will delete it.

## 10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as the **EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)**, the **UK GDPR and
Data Protection Act 2018**, the **California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act
(CCPA/CPRA)** and **Nepal's Individual Privacy Act, 2075 (2018)** give you rights over your personal data, such as
the right to know what is held, to access, correct or delete it, to restrict or object to its use, to data
portability, and not to be discriminated against for using these rights.

- **The app itself:** your settings and games stay under your own control on your device (section 5). The usage
  statistics (section 3) are linked only to a random app-instance ID, not to your name or email; uninstalling the app or
  clearing its data starts a new ID. You may ask us to delete Firebase data or object to its use by writing to us.
- **Emails you sent us:** you may ask for a copy, a correction or deletion at any time by writing to
  poudelbijayraj500@gmail.com. We will reply within one month (GDPR / UK GDPR) or 45 days (CCPA/CPRA).
- **California (CCPA/CPRA):** in the last 12 months we have not sold or "shared" (for cross-context behavioural
  advertising) any personal information, and we do not use sensitive personal information. Through the app we receive
  only the usage statistics in section 3 (an app-instance ID, device and usage information, and approximate country);
  by email, identifiers and message content you choose to send.
- **Nepal (Individual Privacy Act, 2075):** through the app we process only the usage statistics in section 3, for the
  purpose stated there. Information you send us by email is used only for the purpose you sent it for (answering you), is not
  disclosed to anyone else without your consent except as required by law, and is deleted as described in section 8.
- **Complaints:** you may also complain to your local data protection authority (for example your EU supervisory
  authority or the UK Information Commissioner's Office). We would appreciate the chance to answer your concern first.

## 11. Security

device through the app. The settings
and saved game are kept in the app's private storage, protected by your device's own security. Keep your device
updated and locked.
