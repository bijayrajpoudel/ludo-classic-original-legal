### Google Play In-App Review library (Android app only)

From the version after 1.2.14 the Android app includes Google's **Play In-App Review library**, which lets Google Play
show its own rating card inside the game (after a win, at most once every 60 days). It is added to the Android build
by `Assets/LudoImperia/Editor/PlayReviewDependency.cs` and fetched from Google's Maven repository. These libraries are
**not open source**: they are provided by Google under Google's own terms.

| Library | Version | Licence / terms |

| com.google.android.play:core-common (needed by the above) | 2.0.4 | Play Core Software Development Kit Terms of Service, https://developer.android.com/guide/playcore/license |
| com.google.android.gms:play-services-tasks (needed by the above) | 18.2.0 | Android Software Development Kit License, https://developer.android.com/studio/terms |
| com.google.android.gms:play-services-basement (needed by the above) | 18.4.0 | Android Software Development Kit License, https://developer.android.com/studio/terms |
| AndroidX libraries that play-services-basement needs (androidx.core, androidx.fragment, androidx.collection; the Unity Android player already uses AndroidX: appcompat 1.6.1, core 1.9.0, games-activity 4.4.0) | as resolved by Gradle | Apache License 2.0, https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0 |

The rating card itself is shown and handled by the Google Play Store app, under Google's terms and privacy policy; what
a player types in it goes to Google Play as a public review (see Google's note on the library's data at
https://developer.android.com/guide/playcore/in-app-review). The library adds no Android permission to the app (checked in the merged manifest: vibration only, as before) and one
hidden, not-exported helper screen (com.google.android.play.core.common.PlayCoreDialogWrapperActivity).
"Google Play" is a trademark of Google LLC.
