# Privacy Policy — Zenith Wings: Galaxy Shooter

_Effective date: 22 October 2026 · Applies to app version 1.0.0 and later_

**In short:** Zenith Wings is an offline space shooter game. It has no accounts, no ads, no in-app purchases, no
analytics and no tracking. The app does not collect, send, sell or share any personal information, and it does not
even have permission to use the internet. Your progress and settings stay on your own device.

## 1. Who we are

The app is made and published by **Bijay Raj Poudel**, an individual developer in Nepal ("we", "us"). On Google Play
the developer name is **Roulette Wheel**. Contact: **poudelbijayraj500@gmail.com**.

For the purposes of data protection laws (such as the EU and UK GDPR), we would be the "controller" of any personal
data we receive. In practice, the only personal data we can ever receive is what you choose to send us by email
(section 7).

## 2. What this policy covers

This policy covers the Zenith Wings: Galaxy Shooter app on Android (package `com.bijayraj.zenithwings`). It does not
cover app stores, your device's operating system, or apps the game opens for you (section 6), which have their own
privacy policies.

## 3. Information the app does NOT collect

The app:
- has no accounts, sign-in or sign-up;
- does not ask for or read your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, photos, files, camera or
  microphone;
- contains no advertising, analytics, tracking, attribution, social or crash-reporting SDKs;
- does not use the advertising ID or any other device identifier;
- has no online play, chat or online leaderboards: every level is played on your device;
- does not request the Android network ("INTERNET") permission, so it cannot send information about you or your
  games to us or to anyone else.

## 4. Information saved only on your device

So that you can continue where you left off, the app saves the following **only on your device**:
- your progress: unlocked levels, stars and best scores, coins, ship upgrades, which ships you own and which one is
  selected;
- the daily reward streak (the date of your last claim);
- your settings: sound, music and vibration;
- small flags the game uses for itself, such as which tips you have already seen and whether the in-game rating card
  has already been shown.

This data is not sent to us or to anyone else. We cannot see it.

**How long it is kept:** until you clear the app's storage in your phone's settings or uninstall the app.
Uninstalling deletes everything the app saved.

**Device backups:** if you have turned on your phone's own backup service (for example Google's Android backup), your
phone may include the app's saved progress in your personal backup. That backup is handled by your device and its
provider under your account and settings; we never receive it.

## 5. Permissions

- **Vibration** — a short buzz when your ship is hit, a boss explodes or you unlock something. You can switch it off
  in Settings. It gives the app no access to any information.

The app asks for no other permissions. It does not request internet, location, contacts, storage, camera or
microphone access.

## 6. Buttons that leave the app

Some buttons hand you over to other apps, and only when you tap them:
- **RATE ON GOOGLE PLAY** (shown after you choose 4 or 5 stars in the game's rating card, or from Settings) opens the
  app's page in the Google Play Store;
- **SEND FEEDBACK** opens your own email app with our address filled in; you decide whether to send anything.

The star rating you tap inside the game is not sent anywhere; it only decides which of these two buttons is offered.
What happens in other apps is covered by their own privacy policies (for example Google's), not by this one. The app
does not learn what you do there.

## 7. If you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address, your name if your email shows it, and whatever you write. We use it
only to answer you and to fix problems you report. We do not add you to a mailing list, sell it or share it. We keep
the conversation only as long as needed to help you, and in any case delete it within 24 months unless the law
requires otherwise; you can ask us to delete it sooner. Our email is hosted by Google (Gmail), so the message is
stored on Google's servers, which may be outside your country.

Legal basis (GDPR / UK GDPR): our legitimate interest in answering your message (Article 6(1)(f)), or taking steps you
asked for (Article 6(1)(b)).

## 8. Children

The game is rated for ages 7 and up (PEGI 7 / ESRB Everyone) for mild fantasy violence between spaceships, and our
Google Play target audience is 13 and over. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects
none from children under 13 (US COPPA), under 16 (EU GDPR, depending on the country) or of any other age.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has sent us personal information by email, contact us and we
will delete it.

## 9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as the **EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)**, the **UK GDPR and
Data Protection Act 2018**, the **California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act
(CCPA/CPRA)** and **Nepal's Individual Privacy Act, 2075 (2018)** give you rights over your personal data, such as
the right to know what is held, to access, correct or delete it, to restrict or object to its use, to data
portability, and not to be discriminated against for using these rights.

- **The app itself:** we hold no personal data from the app, so there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or
  delete. All game data is under your own control on your device (section 4).
- **Emails you sent us:** you may ask for a copy, a correction or deletion at any time by writing to
  poudelbijayraj500@gmail.com. We will reply within one month (GDPR / UK GDPR) or 45 days (CCPA/CPRA).
- **California (CCPA/CPRA):** in the last 12 months we have not sold or "shared" (for cross-context behavioural
  advertising) any personal information, and we do not use sensitive personal information.
- **Nepal (Individual Privacy Act, 2075):** we do not collect, store, process or disclose personal information through
  the app. Information you send us by email is used only for the purpose you sent it for (answering you).
- **Complaints:** you may also complain to your local data protection authority. We would appreciate the chance to
  answer your concern first.

## 10. Security

No personal data leaves your device through the app, so it cannot be exposed by a breach of our systems. Your saved
progress is kept in the app's private storage, protected by your device's own security.

## 11. Advertising and purchases

The app has no advertising and no in-app purchases. If we ever add ads, purchases or any feature that collects data,
we will update this policy, the store's data safety information and, where required, ask for your consent **before**
that version is released.

## 12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The new version will be published at the same address with a new
effective date.
